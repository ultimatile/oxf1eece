# Third-party data in `lexicon.tsv.gz`

`lexicon.tsv.gz` is generated by `scripts/build_lexicon.py` from the two sources below.
It is a modified, reduced derivative of both: it keeps only single-word lowercase ASCII
entries, at most two definitions per (lemma, part of speech), and ESDB data of size 80 or
less, joined into one table.

## Open English WordNet 2025

- Source: https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet, release `2025-edition`,
  file `english-wordnet-2025.xml.gz`
- Used for: lemmas, parts of speech, definitions
- Attribution: derived from Princeton WordNet and the Open English Wordnet, by the
  Open English Wordnet team
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, with the Princeton WordNet
  licence for the underlying WordNet data. Full texts: `OEWN-LICENSE.md`,
  `OEWN-WNDB_License.txt`

## English Speller Database (ESDB, formerly SCOWL)

- Source: https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist, commit
  `1e5b7d3a72f47a71da5d28686c1dd4b397178485`, file `data/scowl-pre.txt`
- Used for: word size (commonness), inflected forms
- Copyright 2000-2026 by Kevin Atkinson. Licence and the notices of its sources:
  `ESDB-Copyright.txt` (the UKACD section does not apply, since no data above size 80
  is used)
